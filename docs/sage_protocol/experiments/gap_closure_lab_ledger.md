## Stop Decision

Stop condition 1 was met for this experimental branch's targeted high-fit objective: the recombined recency/day/contact portfolio beat the matched no-SAGE control on both 250 and 500 with safe behavior and clear natural helper contribution. A later broad500 top-tool-combo stress run was positive but did not beat the documented current-code best3 500 run-vs-control lift. Minor positive tools are not discarded; `days_between_timestamps`, `relative_day_time_to_timestamp`, `resolve_search_window_or_bounds`, `select_message_content_by_recency`, `select_message_counterparty_for_contact_update`, and the narrowed contact-scalar planners remain retained experimental candidates. The active next step is an iterative generated-tool loop targeting side-effect-free action/precondition feasibility and final action-spec normalization for the largest unsupported broad buckets.
